Why it matters

Hunger and malnutrition hold back millions of Nigerians.

40%

of children under five in Nigeria are stunted, up from 37% in 2018.

8 in 10

Nigerians could not afford a healthy diet in 2025. That is about 191 million people.

2 million

children have severe acute malnutrition, and only about 2 in 10 receive treatment.

How far Nigeria is from the global benchmark
Population experiencing food insecurity

At 77% in 2025, Nigeria is very far from meeting the Global Benchmark of 5.8%. An annual improvement of 26.5% is required to meet the benchmark by 2030.

Population that cannot afford a healthy diet

At 81% in 2025, Nigeria is very far from meeting the Global Benchmark of 2.0%. An annual improvement of 36.7% is required to meet the benchmark by 2030.

Prevalence of undernourishment

At 22% in 2025, Nigeria is very far from meeting the Global Benchmark of 3.3%. An annual improvement of 18.3% is required to meet the benchmark by 2030.

What is the global benchmark? A realistic standard set by the world's top-performing countries. It shows what is achievable today with current technology and resources, rather than the ideal.

Sources: Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey 2023–24 (stunting); UN State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2026 (healthy diet); UNICEF Nigeria, May 2025 (severe acute malnutrition); Food Systems Countdown Initiative (benchmark figures).
