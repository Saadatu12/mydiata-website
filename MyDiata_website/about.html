Our purpose

Grounded in evidence. Designed for action.

Good nutrition depends on good information and skilled people. MyDiata exists to provide both. We do careful research and practical training for health workers, food businesses and organisations, and run programmes that reach families where they live.

Who we are

A Nigerian nutrition centre that works with communities and organisations.

MyDiata Limited is a registered Nigerian company led by nutrition professionals. We help food businesses, NGOs, hospitals, schools and funders understand what people eat, what they need and what works.

We do this in two ways. We work with organisations, through research, consumer research, training and events. And we work directly with communities, through screening, food demonstrations, food assistance and workshops.

How we work

Four things guide every piece of work.

Practical evidence

We collect and use evidence that helps people make real decisions, not reports that sit on a shelf.

Cultural relevance

We design our work around local foods, customs and languages, so it makes sense to the people it is for.

Clarity

We explain findings and advice in plain words that families, field staff and decision makers can follow.

Responsible scope

We stay within our professional scope and say plainly what we know and what we do not.

Our work

What we have done so far.

We have held the Nutrition Innovation Expo, run community outreaches and malnutrition screening, given food demonstrations, provided food assistance and micronutrient powder for mothers of children aged 6 to 23 months, and trained teams on MIYCN and IMAM.
